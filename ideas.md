# Reference Fidelity Specification

## Ground-Truth Design Source

The supplied portfolio at `https://my-portfolio-teal-one-43.vercel.app/` is the ground-truth visual specification for this redesign. The revised website should closely mirror its **light, spacious, developer-forward presentation** rather than the previously selected editorial direction.

## Visual System to Reproduce

The page uses a clean **white / nearly white canvas**, deep navy-black display text, and a saturated royal-blue call-to-action color. Its navigation is light, compact, and horizontally centred around a small `ala.` wordmark. The hero is a broad three-column composition: bio and contact actions at left, an actual portrait fading into the pale background at centre, and a vertical stack of raised rounded role cards at right. Thin pale-blue connecting lines and small node circles provide the technical decoration.

Section headings are large, dark, and left aligned. Each section has considerable vertical breathing room and a tiny all-caps blue overline with a short horizontal rule. Surfaces use soft shadows, generous border radii, light blue-tinted backgrounds, compact blue chips, and approachable product-card presentation. The revised experience should feel polished, modern, and personable—not editorial, paper-textured, or high-contrast.

## Required Layout Details

The revised site will preserve the reference sequence: fixed-style top navigation, portrait-led hero, simple about introduction with numbered stats, experience timeline, categorized skill grid, full-stack capability band, project cards, and a clear contact close. Project and hero panels will use rounded corners, pale blue backgrounds, dark text, and familiar blue action elements.

## Fidelity Decisions

The actual portrait supplied in the reference site has been saved as `/home/ubuntu/webdev-static-assets/ala-reference-portrait.webp` for use in the revised hero. Existing biography, experience, skills, projects, email, phone, and social content will remain the user's own.

## Style Decisions

- Use a light palette of white, pale cloud-blue, muted slate, navy-black, and royal blue.
- Use a bold humanist display typeface with a friendly, rounded sans serif body typeface.
- Use controlled shadow, radius, connectors, and blue indicator dots as the recurring visual language.
- Remove warm paper, dossier styling, technical grids, and all previous cobalt-accented editorial treatments.
